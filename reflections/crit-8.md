# Crit 8 reflection

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

The breakthrough came when I was thinking of services that I had recently used and had a bad experience. I thought about the BOM website and the urgent questions that people bring to it on a daily basis: what's the weather where I am, is rain coming, and am I in danger? Once I thought about that, the 'good' version became obvious. I immediately came up with ideas like: the site should load the user's current location immediately, so the forecast, radar and any active warnings appear without a single click, and search becomes a way to check somewhere else rather than the starting point. etc.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

I think this project further changed how I think about who i'm building for. It's easy to design for someone like me on fast internet, comfortable with technology, and checking the weather out of curiosity. But the people who depend on BOM can be farmers deciding whether to move stock, a family in a cyclone's path, or someone in a regional town with bad reception. I want to be a developer who gets close to or thinks about real users before writing any code. That means asking what they're trying to do, watching where they get stuck, and treating complaints as research rather than noise.
